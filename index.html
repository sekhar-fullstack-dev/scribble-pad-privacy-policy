Scribble Pad

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 7, 2026


✔ No personal data collected

Scribble Pad ("the app") is developed by AutisticDev. This policy explains what information the app does and does not collect, and how it operates with respect to your privacy.

Who This App Is For

Scribble Pad is designed for children aged 4–8 to practice writing letters, numbers, and basic math. We take children's privacy seriously and have built the app to collect no personal information whatsoever.

Data We Do Not Collect

The app does not collect, transmit, or share:

  • Names, email addresses, or any identifying information
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Handwriting or drawing data
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Any data from children

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The app saves the child's learning progress (practice scores per letter, number, and math operation) to the device's local storage using Android's standard preferences API. This data:

  • Never leaves the device
  • Is not accessible to AutisticDev or any third party
  • Can be cleared at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing app data in device settings

Handwriting Recognition

The app uses Google ML Kit Digital Ink Recognition to check the child's handwriting. This runs entirely on-device. No handwriting strokes or drawing data are sent to Google servers or any external service.

Text-to-Speech

Letter and word prompts are spoken aloud using the device's built-in text-to-speech engine. This also runs on-device and sends no data externally.

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate any advertising networks, analytics platforms, social media SDKs, or payment processors. No third-party service receives any data from this app.

Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Scribble Pad complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. Since the app collects no data at all, there is nothing to delete or request.

Changes to This Policy

If we make any changes to this privacy policy, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
